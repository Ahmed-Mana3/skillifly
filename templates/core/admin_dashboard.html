  • Signups are editor accounts (not clients, school admins or the demo account) created in the period. What became of them is measured today, so recent periods look lower until people have had time to build and pay.
  • Added a first video is the moment a portfolio becomes worth sharing. Finished setup means all four launch-checklist steps (name and title, a video, a theme, skills).
  • Money numbers are the same as on Revenue insights: revenue is confirmed payments above zero; MRR spreads each running paid plan over 30 days; renewed means paid again within 14 days of the plan ending.
  • Active is the latest of last seen, last login and signup. Only the latest visit is stored, so active users have no comparison with an earlier period.
