Is Skillifly really free?{% include "core/landing/_icon.html" with i="plus" %}
Yes. You can build, customize and preview your whole portfolio for free, with no card. You only pay when you want to publish it on a public link that clients can open.
What do I get with Pro?{% include "core/landing/_icon.html" with i="plus" %}
Your live public portfolio at skillifly.cloud/you, visitor analytics, your own custom domain with SSL, SEO settings, the Skillifly AI assistant, more upload storage, early access to new themes and priority support.
Can I upload videos, or only use links?{% include "core/landing/_icon.html" with i="plus" %}
Both. Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, or upload your video files directly to Skillifly. Reels and long-form videos each get their own page.
Can I change my theme later?{% include "core/landing/_icon.html" with i="plus" %}
Any time, in one click. Your projects, skills and text move over to the new design automatically.
Does it work in Arabic?{% include "core/landing/_icon.html" with i="plus" %}
Yes. Skillifly is fully bilingual — you can use the whole platform in Arabic or English, with right-to-left layouts built in.
How do I pay?{% include "core/landing/_icon.html" with i="plus" %}
Pay locally with InstaPay or Vodafone Cash and upload your receipt. Our AI checks it so your plan is activated fast. Prices are in EGP.
What happens if my plan ends?{% include "core/landing/_icon.html" with i="plus" %}
Nothing is deleted. Your portfolio switches to private until you renew, then it's back on the same link with everything in place.
Is it only for video editors?{% include "core/landing/_icon.html" with i="plus" %}
Skillifly is designed for video editors first, and it works just as well for motion designers, videographers, colorists and other creatives who sell with their work.
