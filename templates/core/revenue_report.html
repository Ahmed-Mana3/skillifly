  • Revenue is confirmed payments above zero. Free passes (100% coupons, school codes, admin grants) are counted separately and never add to revenue. Receipts still waiting for a check are not included.
  • MRR spreads what each customer paid for their running plan over its length, per 30 days. When someone renews early, only their newest paid plan counts, so nobody is counted twice. ARR is MRR × 12.
  • Renewed means the customer paid again before the plan ended or within {{ grace_days }} days after. Plans that ended less than {{ grace_days }} days ago without a payment are undecided and left out of the rate.
  • Renewals due use the price each customer last paid, so the forecast is conservative. "Expect" applies the all-time renewal rate once at least 5 plans have ended.
  • Discounts compare against today's list prices (Monthly EGP {{ prices.monthly }}, Annual EGP {{ prices.annual }}). Older payments made at a different list price show as discounted.
  • Signups who paid counts editor accounts created in the period that have paid by now, so recent periods look lower until people have had time to decide.
  • Only data from {{ min_date|date:"j M Y" }} on. Earlier payments are launch-era test data. A comparison is only shown when the whole previous period falls after this date. The checkout funnel is on Payment performance.
