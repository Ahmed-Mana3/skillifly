How these numbers are worked out
  • Last active is the latest of: any signed-in page view, a login, or the signup itself. Only the latest time is stored, so “active users” is exact for the period, but can't be compared with an earlier period.
  • Came back means active again at least a day after signing up. Left on day one means more than 30 days since signup and never back after that first day. Lapsed means they came back at least once, then nothing for 30+ days.
  • Paying means a running plan and at least one payment with money. Coupon, school and admin-granted plans are Free pass. Plan values use today's prices (Monthly / Annual).
  • Paying customers change is measured against how many were paying at the start of the period.
  • Portfolio visits come from the portfolio tracker: bots and owners viewing their own portfolio are not counted. The theme-preview demo account is never included.
  • Signup comparisons use today's state of both groups, so the older group has had longer to set up and come back.
