Note: themes usually center sections with `margin: 0 auto; max-width: Npx`. In a flex container, auto cross-axis margins disable `align-items: stretch` and would collapse each section to its content width, so direct children get an explicit `width: 100%` here (their `max-width` + auto margins still keep them centered on wide viewports).
